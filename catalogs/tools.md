# Social management tools (10)

Curated, real products. Official sites only. social-agent does not bundle or
resell any of these — they are alternatives/complements for teams that want a
hosted dashboard instead of (or alongside) this toolkit.

1. **Buffer** — https://buffer.com — scheduling + analytics, generous free tier.
2. **Hootsuite** — https://www.hootsuite.com — streams, scheduling, team workflows.
3. **Sprout Social** — https://sproutsocial.com — enterprise scheduling + CRM-ish inbox.
4. **Later** — https://later.com — visual planner, strong on Instagram/TikTok.
5. **Loomly** — https://www.loomly.com — calendar-first collaboration.
6. **Metricool** — https://metricool.com — scheduling + analytics + ad tracking.
7. **SocialBee** — https://socialbee.com — category-based evergreen scheduling.
8. **Sendible** — https://www.sendible.com — agency-focused, client dashboards.
9. **Agorapulse** — https://www.agorapulse.com — unified inbox + publishing.
10. **CoSchedule** — https://coschedule.com — marketing calendar with social.

Count: 10 entries, all official product sites.
