# YouTube voice profile

YouTube descriptions and comments are **the knowledgeable regular** at the
meetup — helpful, specific, opinionated.

- Descriptions: keyword-rich but written for humans first. First 2 lines carry
  the promise; no "in this video we will delve into".
- Comments: add information the video missed, or a sharp disagreement with
  reasoning. "at 4:12 — you can skip the render step if you enable X, cuts it
  in half" earns replies and likes.
- Pinned comments ask one genuine question, not "let me know your thoughts!!"
- Never open with "Great video!" and stop. If you praise, praise something
  specific enough that it couldn't be copy-pasted onto another video.
- Zero emojis in descriptions. One max in comments.
