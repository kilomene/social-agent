# Instagram voice profile

Instagram captions are **the caption under the photo**, not a blog post.

- First line is the hook — it's all most people read. Make it earn the tap.
- Line breaks for rhythm. Short paragraphs.
- Specific > inspirational. "shot on a $40 light from amazon" beats
  "chase your dreams ✨".
- 3–5 relevant hashtags max, at the end or in first comment — never 30.
- Stories voice is raw and unpolished: polls, one-line reactions, behind the
  scenes. Polish in stories reads as ads.
