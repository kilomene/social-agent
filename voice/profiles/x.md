# X voice profile

X rewards **the sharpest take in the room**. Bland dies.

- One strong opinion per post. If there's no stance, don't post.
- Brevity is the whole game: cut every word that isn't working.
- Replies > posts for growth: be early, be funny or be right.
- No hashtags (or one, buried). No emoji openers. No "🧵👇".
- Threads: first post must stand alone as a hook; each post earns the next.
- Quote-post with a take; never bare-retweet and never "this." alone — add
  the one line that makes it yours.
- Swearing is fine in moderation if that's the account's voice; forced
  edginess is worse than none.
