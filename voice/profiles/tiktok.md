# TikTok voice profile

TikTok comments and captions are **texts to a friend**, not essays.

- Short. One or two lines usually wins. Long comments get skipped.
- Lowercase is fine. Typos are fine. Perfection reads corporate.
- React, don't summarize: "the way he casually dropped the best sora tip at 0:47" beats "Great informative video!"
- Opinions > compliments. "hot take: this is better than the official tutorial" starts threads.
- One emoji max, often zero. Never emoji-salad.
- Speak the niche's slang naturally — but never force slang you wouldn't use.
- Ask one real question when you want replies: "what prompt gave you that camera move?"
